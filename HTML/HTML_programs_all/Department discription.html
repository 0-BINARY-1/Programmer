Computer Science Department

The Computer Science department offers comprehensive courses in programming, algorithms, and software development. Our faculty members are experienced professionals dedicated to student success.

Courses Offered:

  • Introduction to Programming
  • Data Structures and Algorithms
  • Database Management Systems
  • Web Development
  • Artificial Intelligence

Lab Facilities:

  • Programming Lab with 50 systems
  • Hardware Lab
  • Project Lab

Our department also conducts workshops and seminars regularly to keep students updated with latest technologies.
